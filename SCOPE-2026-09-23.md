### BC-13 design scope (M-02)

`step_executor.py:536-537` awaits a worker-thread future with a timeout;
on `FutureTimeoutError` it calls `future.cancel()` — a no-op for a running
thread (:544) — then emits a subprocess-shaped receipt (exit -1, FAILED,
partial stdout, honest stderr notice at :552-557). The abandoned thread
keeps writing artifacts and mutating shared process state (matplotlib Agg
for steps 7/8 per ADR 0001 slice-D), its post-timeout output is dropped,
and under `--parallel` it holds a worker slot indefinitely. Design
options: (a) move whitelisted steps to subprocess workers (kill wins
force-kill back), or (b) cooperative CancelToken checked at safe points,
mirroring `run_subprocess_envelope`'s token (lean is the documented
holdout, executor.py:293-294). Receipt must add `force_killed:
true|false`, keep `execution_mode: consolidated`, mirror
`step_timeouts` env overrides (`GNN_STEP_TIMEOUT_{N}`/`_SCALE`), and
mark partial artifacts mid-write. Extend tests/pipeline/test_step_executor.py
+ test_step_timeouts.py.
